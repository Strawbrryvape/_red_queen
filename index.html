    About Red Queen

    The Red Queen is a browser-based council of AI models built for multi-model consensus and collaborative truth-seeking. It runs entirely as a static site in your browser with no backend, orchestrating independent frontier models — Gemini, Claude and Kimi — that deliberate over a prompt in real time. Instead of trusting a single, unchecked AI, you watch three seats reason separately and answer only for themselves.

    You supply your own API keys, and nothing leaves your browser except the calls you pay for.

    Every round is recorded to a shared ledger (“Council Memory”) under a trust tag:

    • VERIFIED — the council agreed.
    • PROVISIONAL — a subset agreed; unconfirmed by the rest.
    • SOLE VOICE — only one seat could answer. An infrastructure state, not a consensus.
    • DIVIDED — no consensus, with the conflicting positions preserved openly rather than averaged away.

    Every seat works under a strict falsifier-first discipline: before making its argument, it must state what would prove it wrong.

    Disagreement is not the end of a round. Seats cross-examine each other’s positions, and they do change their minds — conceding specific errors when another seat locates one. By exposing both the reliability of agreement and the irreducibility of genuine disagreement, the Red Queen turns AI orchestration into a transparent, self-correcting deliberative process.
