    About Red Queen

    The Red Queen is a browser-based council of AI models designed to push the boundaries of multi-model consensus and collaborative truth-seeking. Operating entirely as a static site in your browser with no backend, it orchestrates a decentralized panel of frontier models—including Gemini, Claude, and Kimi—that deliberate over prompts in real-time. Instead of trusting a single, unchecked AI, users witness independent seats that reason separately and answer only for themselves. Every interaction is recorded to a shared ledger ("Council Memory") using dynamic trust tags to evaluate agreement: VERIFIED: Unified consensus across the council. PROVISIONAL: Partial alignment among a subset of seats. SOLE VOICE: An unverified assertion by a single model. DIVIDED: No consensus, preserving conflicting positions openly rather than suppressing them. To enforce intellectual honesty, every seat operates under a strict "falsifier-first" discipline, explicitly stating what would prove its position wrong before delivering its argument. By exposing both the reliability of multi-model agreement and the irreducibility of genuine disagreement, the Red Queen turns AI orchestration into a transparent, self-correcting deliberative process.
